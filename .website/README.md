# 🌈POOP N’ SCOOP

Welcome to Poop n’ Scoop, the most magical ice cream shop in town!

Your seven rainbow unicorns are producing delicious soft serve. Serve as many hungry customers as you can!

But watch out! Health inspectors, dropped scoops, and pesky flies can turn your magical shop into a disaster!

## 🕹️CONTROLS

| ACTION | KEYBOARD | CONTROLLER |
|---|---|---|
| **MOVE** | <kbd>↑</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd><kbd>←</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> | LEFT ANALOG STICK |
| **SCOOP / SERVE** | <kbd>SPACEBAR</kbd> | A or ✕ |
| **DROP SCOOP** | <kbd>X</kbd> | X or ☐ |
| **START** | <kbd>N</kbd> | ☰ |
| **MUTE** | <kbd>M</kbd> | Y or △ |

## 🦄SERVING CUSTOMERS

Customers appear along the counter with their orders.

Grab the correct colors from the unicorns and deliver them in the exact order requested. Serve a complete order while the customer is still **HAPPY** and you'll earn a **TIP!**

Each round, customers move faster and demand more scoops. Keep them happy and keep the scoops coming!

## 🪰WATCH OUT!

**HEALTH INSPECTOR**  
The Health Inspector will charge you $3 if he passes over a dropped scoop. Get caught carrying unicorn poop and you’ll be fined $10 and lose your cone!

**FLIES**  
Drop too many scoops and you’ll attract FLIES! They’ll contaminate the cone you’re carrying. Drop contaminated scoops and grab a fresh one before serving!

**CODE BROWN**  
In later rounds, unicorns may drop a CODE BROWN! It’s plain old poop—not soft serve! Drop it and grab a fresh scoop before serving!

## 😞GAME OVER

Hungry customers won't wait forever! If a customer reaches the far end of the counter without being served, it's **GAME OVER MAN!**

Serve quickly. Match those colors. Don't get caught serving unicorn poop.
